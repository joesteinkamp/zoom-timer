Meeting Timer

A countdown for Zoom meetings that everyone can see, with a chime everyone can hear.

Installing

Add Meeting Timer from the Zoom App Marketplace. After you approve the permissions, the app opens in your Zoom client. It appears in the Apps panel during any meeting.

You need the Zoom desktop client on Windows or macOS. Version 5.17.5 or later supports everything the app does; on 5.12.6 to 5.17.4 the chime works but the shared countdown badge does not. Mobile and web clients can run the timer for you alone — Zoom does not offer audio sharing or the countdown badge there.

Running a timer

  • Open Apps in a meeting and choose Meeting Timer.
  • Pick one of the preset durations, or type your own — 7 for seven minutes, 7:30, 90s, or 2m30s.
  • While it runs you can pause, add a minute, or reset. Adding a minute extends the badge everyone sees, too.

What other participants see and hear

Two separate things happen, and they can succeed independently:

The countdown badgeA live countdown appears next to your name in every participant's client, drawn by Zoom itself. It needs no screen share and no permission, and it works whether or not the chime does.
The chimeAt zero, the app turns on Zoom's share computer audio for the length of the chime so the meeting can hear it, then turns it straight back off.

What the audio notice is about. While the share is open — roughly two and a half seconds — everything audible on your computer goes into the meeting, not only the chime. A notification sound or a video playing in another window would be heard too. The app shows you this once before your first countdown.

When the chime won't be heard

The app tells you which of these applies before you start a timer, so it is never a surprise at zero. In all of them, the countdown badge still works.

SituationWhat happens
You are already sharing your screen with computer sound on The chime is already audible; the app leaves your share alone.
You are sharing your screen without sound The app offers a one-click shortcut to restart the share with sound enabled.
Someone else is sharing Zoom gives the audio channel to their share. You hear the chime locally; everyone still sees the countdown. If your host has enabled multiple participants can share simultaneously, this may not apply.
Your host or admin has disabled computer audio sharing The chime plays for you only, and the app says so before you start.

Frequently asked

Can I use a different sound? Not yet. The app ships one chime, which is the reason it exists — Zoom's own countdown can only play sounds from its built-in list.

Does it record anything? No. The app has no access to meeting audio, video, chat, or recordings, and stores nothing about you. See the Privacy Policy.

Does the timer drift if I switch away? No. It works from a fixed end time rather than counting down, so a backgrounded panel catches up the moment it wakes.

Can other participants control it? No. Only you run the timer; everyone else sees the countdown.
