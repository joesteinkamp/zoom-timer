Support

Something not working? Start here — most of it comes down to which Zoom client you are on, or who else is sharing.

Common problems

What you seeWhat it means
"This client cannot share audio, so only you will hear the chime." You are on mobile, on the web client, or on a desktop client older than 5.12.6. Audio sharing is a desktop feature. Update the desktop client, or use the countdown badge alone.
Participants hear nothing at zero, and no message explained why Check whether computer audio sharing is disabled for your account or meeting — an administrator can turn it off centrally.
"Someone else is sharing, so only you may hear the chime." Zoom gives the audio channel to whoever is sharing. Ask the host to enable multiple participants can share simultaneously, or run the timer while nobody is sharing.
No countdown appears next to your name The badge needs desktop client 5.17.5 or later. The timer and chime still work without it.
The panel is blank Close and reopen the app from the Apps panel. If it persists, restart the Zoom client — the app is a webview and occasionally needs one.
The chime is quiet for other participants Shared computer audio carries your system volume. Turn your own volume up before the timer ends.

Requirements

  • Zoom desktop client for Windows or macOS.
  • 5.12.6 or later for the chime; 5.17.5 or later for the shared countdown badge.
  • Permission to share audio in the meeting — in a webinar, that means host, co-host, or panelist.

Reporting a problem

Open an issue on GitHub. What helps most:

  • Your Zoom client version and operating system.
  • Whether you were in a meeting or a webinar, and your role in it.
  • Whether anyone was sharing a screen at the time.
  • Exactly what the app said, if it showed a message.

Issues are read and answered by the developer. There is no support desk and no ticket number — it is a small free app.
