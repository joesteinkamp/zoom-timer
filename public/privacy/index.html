Meeting Timer

Privacy Policy

Meeting Timer is a countdown timer for Zoom meetings. It has no user accounts, no database, and no analytics. This policy explains what that means in detail.

Last updated 27 August 2026

The short version. Meeting Timer does not store your personal data anywhere. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted to us or to any third party. The only information the app keeps is a countdown length saved in your own browser storage, which never leaves your device.

What the app reads while you use it

When Meeting Timer is open in a Zoom meeting, it asks the Zoom Apps SDK for a small amount of context. Every item below is held in memory for the length of your session and is never written down, sent anywhere, or shared:

WhatWhy the app asks for it
Meeting topicLabels the countdown badge, so participants see what the timer is for
Your role in the meetingZoom only allows panelists, co-hosts, and hosts to share audio in a webinar; the app checks before offering to
Your display name and participant IDDistinguishes a screen share you started from one someone else started, so the app never closes a share that isn't its own
Zoom client version and running contextDetects which features this client supports, so unavailable ones are not offered

What the app stores on your device

  • Your last countdown length, so the app can offer it again next time.
  • Your acknowledgement of the audio notice, so you are shown it once rather than every session.

Both live in your browser's local storage inside the Zoom client, on your device only. Clearing your Zoom app data or removing the app removes them. Neither is readable by us.

Audio sharing

When a countdown reaches zero, the app briefly turns on Zoom's share computer audio so other participants can hear the chime. While that share is open — the length of the chime, about two and a half seconds — all sound coming out of your computer is audible to the meeting, not only the chime. The app opens the share immediately before the chime and closes it as soon as playback ends.

The audio itself is handled entirely by Zoom. Meeting Timer does not record, receive, store, or transmit any audio, and it never accesses your microphone or camera.

Installation

Installing the app runs a standard Zoom OAuth flow. During it:

  • A short-lived cookie holds a random anti-forgery value for up to ten minutes. It is signed, marked HttpOnly and Secure, contains no personal information, and is cleared as soon as the install completes.
  • Zoom issues an access token, which is used once to ask Zoom for the link that opens the app in your client, and is then discarded. It is never written to disk or to a database.

What we never do

  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, or advertising.
  • No cookies other than the ten-minute install cookie described above.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of personal data — there is none to sell.
  • No access to meeting content: no recordings, transcripts, chat, participant lists, or video.

Service providers

The app's pages and its two installation functions are hosted on Vercel, which processes standard web server request data such as IP address and user agent in order to serve the page. Zoom operates the meeting and the client the app runs inside. No other third party is involved.

Retention, access, and deletion

Because no personal data is collected or stored, there is nothing to retain and nothing to export or erase on request. To remove the two values the app saved in your own browser, remove the app from Zoom or clear your Zoom app data.

Removing the app also notifies us through Zoom's deauthorization webhook. That notification is acknowledged and no data is deleted, because none was ever held.

Children

Meeting Timer is a workplace tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Material changes will be noted on the app's Marketplace listing.
