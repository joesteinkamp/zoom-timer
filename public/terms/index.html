2. What the app does

Meeting Timer displays a countdown in the Zoom side panel, shows that countdown to other participants using Zoom's dynamic indicator, and plays a chime when the countdown reaches zero. To make the chime audible to other participants, the app briefly enables Zoom's share computer audio feature. While that share is open, all sound from your computer is audible to the meeting. You are shown a notice explaining this before your first countdown, and you control when a countdown runs.
